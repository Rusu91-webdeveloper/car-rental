## Recommended document policy baseline (proposal, not a decided requirement)

Allow one or two files per required document according to front/back policy; PDF, JPEG and PNG only; 10 MB maximum per file; validate declared MIME, magic bytes, decoded dimensions/PDF structure, checksum and malware scan. Store only metadata and opaque object keys in PostgreSQL. Use a private EU-region bucket with provider-managed encryption plus short-lived signed URLs issued only after booking ownership or explicit admin-role checks. Quarantine until scanning completes. Log document access. Delete on a documented retention schedule and support legal holds/subject requests. These numbers and the provider require client/legal approval.
